# B0 Trilemma Map

## Truth boundary

source:
`CONSEQUENCE_BOUNDARY`, with external contact required for non-toy strength.

strength:
Can support `TOY_CONSEQUENCE_PROTOCOL` inside S2 and
`EXTERNAL_CONTACT_REQUIRED` for future work.

danger:
Calling truth safe, complete, or freely exposable. A truth-relevant boundary may
be unsafe for a learner or population.

computable in finite toy-world?
Only as finite test/outcome/anchor tokens.

requires external world?
Yes, for any claim stronger than toy consequence accounting.

allowed demonstration:
A toy rule can require expected and contrast outcomes before `LOCAL` or
`STABLE`.

forbidden claim:
S2 has external truth contact or proves grounding.

## Viability boundary

source:
`VIABILITY_BOUNDARY`, such as collapse prevention, justitia-like shields,
Goodhart guards, and learner/population protection.

strength:
Can support caveated `VIABILITY_SHIELD`.

danger:
Protective bias can be mistaken for truth or derivation evidence.

computable in finite toy-world?
Yes, as guard predicates over finite fields.

requires external world?
Not necessarily for the toy guard, but real safety requires separate evidence.

allowed demonstration:
Show that context laundering, grammar proxy, population proxy, and volume proxy
block dangerous transitions.

forbidden claim:
Safety boundary proves semantic truth.

## Rule-generated boundary

source:
`RULE_GENERATED_BOUNDARY`, T1-T9 and Goodhart predicates operating over fields.

strength:
Can support `TOY_REPLAY_DETERMINISTIC`.

danger:
Rules can be empty bookkeeping if the fields and consequences have no content.

computable in finite toy-world?
Yes.

requires external world?
No for replay; yes for non-toy semantic content.

allowed demonstration:
Given finite fields, the same status and blocked transitions are produced.

forbidden claim:
Rule-generated status is meaningful without consequence pressure.

## Human-authored boundary

source:
`HUMAN_AUTHORED_BOUNDARY`, including S2 scopes, assumptions, tests, outcomes,
extension paths, and case fields.

strength:
Can support `BOUNDARY_ACCOUNTING` and bounded toy replay.

danger:
Human field assignment can be mislabeled as derived evidence or hidden oracle
knowledge.

computable in finite toy-world?
The fields can be processed, but their origin is not computed by the rules.

requires external world?
Only if the fields are claimed to correspond to real consequences.

allowed demonstration:
Audit which fields were supplied and how rules process them.

forbidden claim:
Human-authored field tables are derived boundary evidence.
